About

Ian Trottier

I was born in the Spirit of 76 and raised in the San Francisco Bay Area, fourth generation. I graduated from the University of Oregon in 1999.

In 2007 I founded iPark, which I later sold to Imperial Parking of New York. In 2016 I launched the Discussions of Truth podcast. In 2018 I ran for the U.S. House of Representatives in Florida’s 27th District.

I am the author of Freedom Reserved: No More Lies and High Stakes Treason: How John Brennan Compromised American Security for Millions, published by Bombardier Books in 2025.

This site is where I keep the work — the articles, the documents, and the record.
